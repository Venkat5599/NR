01
Lunar is a
keyless vault contract
for your Bitcoin,
Ethereum, Base and NEAR.
Your coins sit in a NEAR contract
whose access keys are all deleted.
It controls your addresses on every
chain via Chain Signatures,
and your phone holds one key.
It can ask for money and
nothing else.
Small amounts move at once, up to one daily limit across every chain.
Anything bigger waits 72 hours, to a week-old saved address.
Any one of your guardians
can stop it.
Like a shop's time-delay safe,
the goal is simple: make coercion stop paying,
shrinking what a robber can demand
from
everything
to
today's limit
.
Dashboard concept. The interface is in design and not live yet.
